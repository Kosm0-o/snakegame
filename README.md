# Snake 'Em
Snake 'Em is a 2D bossfight game remake of the classic game "Snake". The most popular version of the game "Snake" ran on Nokia phones, before being brought to computers and other devices. It's a game where the player is a snake and they had to get as long as possible by eating fruits that appear randomly around the map. If the snake crashed into it's tail or into a wall the player loses. Snake 'Em takes that same game aspect, but now there are boss(es) and special abilities. Using WASD or the Arrow Keys, as well as Z and X, players must navigate around, and defeat, the spider boss and it's attacks. The player can eat apples to grow and also shoot a fireball. They can also wait for their lightning zap ability to ready up so they can sacrifice 3 segments for a powerful lightning attack (with stun!). The boss only gets harder the more health you take. Can you win?

I made this originally for Hack Club's you-snake-we-snake YSWS. In You Snake We Snake, you were supposed to make a classic snake-inspired game. I thought of a few ideas, but then when I was thinking about my friend's games that he has made, one of them was a Boss Rush. A Boss Rush is a game where you just keep on fighting bosses; one after another. I combined the 2 things and now we have my game. Suprisingly there isn't much of anything that similar to my idea. I just decided to pick a spider as the first boss randomly. My goal was to get faster at polish and better at fx. I'm not very good at vfx so I mostly handle all animations and stuff through code lol. In the original ship, I didn't get much polish and the fighting felt kind of lackluster. I thought I would be done, but then I updated this with an extra 20 hours of work. I got super invested and made some really nice little details that I'm proud of. I feel like the spider feels a lot more alive than before. That being said, there were some bugs that were disgustingly hard to fix, but I think they're all gone (at least that I know of). This game was just overall good for motivation because I was really low on creative juices the week prior. I hope to update it in the future, as I'm working on a big project right now and this was kind of a side quest lol. Future plans: new bosses, new abilities, add roguelite features, MORE POLISHHH.
